Privacy Policy for Auto Scroll Extension

Effective date: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Auto Scroll Extension ("Extension", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use it.

1. Summary

  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.
  • We do not send your browsing data to external servers.
  • Any settings used by the Extension are stored locally in your browser.

2. Information We Access

To provide auto-scrolling functionality, the Extension may run on webpages you visit. This allows it to:

  • Start and stop scrolling on the active page.
  • Read and apply your saved scrolling preferences.

The Extension does not read your personal messages, login credentials, payment details, or form data for analytics or profiling.

3. Information We Store

The Extension may store limited configuration data using your browser's extension storage API, such as:

  • Scroll speed
  • Scroll direction or behavior preferences
  • Toggle states (for example, whether auto-scroll is enabled)

This data remains on your device/browser profile and is used only to make the Extension work as expected.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Personally identifiable information (PII)
  • Browsing history for tracking purposes
  • Page content for analytics
  • Keystrokes or passwords
  • Financial or health data

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

The Extension does not include third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or external telemetry endpoints.

6. Permissions and Why They Are Needed

The Extension requests browser permissions only to deliver its core features:

  • activeTab: To interact with the currently active tab when you use the Extension.
  • scripting: To run scrolling behavior on webpages.
  • storage: To save your Extension preferences locally.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Preference data is retained locally until you change it, clear browser extension data, or uninstall the Extension.
  • Uninstalling the Extension removes its local data according to browser behavior.

8. Security

Because data is kept locally and not transmitted to our servers, exposure risk is reduced. However, no software environment is completely risk-free.

9. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product changes, legal requirements, or improvements. Updates will be reflected by changing the effective date above.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer/publisher of the Auto Scroll Extension through the extension listing or repository support channel.
